## What c15t does

c15t is an open-source consent manager. It shows visitors a banner and a
preference dialog, remembers their choice, and holds back analytics, ads,
pixels and embeds until they allow them. The rules for who sees what come from
a policy: opt-in for visitors in the EU, opt-out for some US states, or
whatever your project configures.

You get:

- Banner, dialog and preferences link components for your framework, with a
  theme system and slots for your design.
- Script loading that waits for consent, for [30+ vendors](/docs/integrations/overview)
  or your own scripts.
- Consent records stored by [Inth](https://inth.com) or a
  [backend you run](/docs/self-host/overview).
- Google Consent Mode v2 and IAB TCF 2.4 support.

A working setup takes four steps in every framework: install c15t, point it at
your Inth project's backend URL, mount the consent components once near the
root of your app, and move your analytics and marketing scripts into c15t so
they wait for consent. Then [verify it](/docs/guides/verify-consent) by
watching network requests, not just the banner.

## Using a coding agent

Each c15t package ships its docs as Markdown. After installing, agents can read
`node_modules/c15t/AGENTS.md` for an index of version-matched guides. On the
website, every page has a Markdown version, and
[llms.txt](https://c15t.com/docs/llms.txt) lists them all.

Tell the agent your framework, router and hosting before it picks a recipe. A
Next.js App Router app on Vercel and a static Astro site need different setups.
