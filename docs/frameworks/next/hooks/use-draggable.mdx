---
title: Draggable controls
description: Use the built-in dialog trigger for a draggable privacy control.
---

`useDraggable` is an internal hook and is not exported by the consent adapter.
Use `ConsentDialogTrigger` for the built-in draggable control or implement dragging
in your host UI. Keep the control accessible before and after a choice and while
a notice is required. See [persistent preferences](/docs/upgrade-v3#keep-notices-local-and-preferences-reachable).
