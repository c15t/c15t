## Before the policy resolves

The plugin starts with every optional category denied and no recorded choice
read. The composables update once the browser has read the stored choice and
the policy arrives. Until then `useConsentSnapshot().value.policyPending` is
`true` and `usePolicyResolution()` reports a status other than `'matched'`,
so you can hold back UI that depends on the answer.

The `language` prop on [`ConsentRoot`](/docs/frameworks/vue/components/consent-root#props)
sets the language and resolves again in one step. See
[translations](/docs/frameworks/vue/translations).
