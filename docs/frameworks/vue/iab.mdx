## Turn on IAB TCF

IAB TCF is a policy setting, not a Vue option. When the visitor's policy uses
the `iab` model and names your CMP ID, `ConsentRoot` renders the IAB banner and
preference centre in place of the standard ones. No extra component or
stylesheet is needed; the IAB components include their own styles. Configure
the CMP ID, vendors and publisher restrictions in your Inth project, or see
[IAB TCF for a self-hosted backend](/docs/self-host/guides/iab-tcf).

To compose the IAB surfaces yourself, import them from
`c15t/vue/runtime/components/iab-consent-banner.vue` and
`c15t/vue/runtime/components/iab-consent-dialog.vue`, and render them in place
of `ConsentRoot`. For a custom preference centre, `useConsentIabSelection()`
reads the visitor's purpose and vendor selection and `useConsentIabSave()`
writes it and the TC String.

## Vendor list loading

The browser receives a reference to the Global Vendor List with the policy.
The IAB module fetches the full list as soon as it mounts, before the visitor
clicks, and waits for it before it writes a TC String. A failed fetch leaves
the banner visible and grants nothing. Allow the list's origin, usually your
backend, in your CSP `connect-src`.

## Verify IAB consent

Load the app under your IAB policy and confirm the IAB banner appears. In the
browser console, `__tcfapi('getTCData', 2, console.log)` returns a TC String
after you accept or reject.
