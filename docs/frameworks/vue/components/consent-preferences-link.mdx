## Import the link into your footer

Import `ConsentPreferencesLink` from
`c15t/vue/runtime/components/consent-preferences-link.vue` and put it in your
footer, next to your privacy policy link:

Pass content in the default slot to change the label. The
[embed example](/docs/frameworks/vue/embeds) uses it inside a placeholder as
"Choose video permissions".
