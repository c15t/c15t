## Inspect consent with DevTools

Add the DevTools tag next to the c15t tag while you debug. The panel shows
consent state, gated scripts, the resolved policy and every event. Its Location
tab changes the country, region, language and GPC signal and runs the policy
again, so you can see what a visitor elsewhere gets.

{/* example: fragment */}

```html
<script
  src="https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/@c15t/browser@alpha/dist/c15t.devtools.js"
  data-open
  data-tab="location"
  defer
></script>
```

Tag order does not matter. `data-tab` picks the first tab: `consents`,
`scripts`, `location`, `policy`, `iab`, `events` or `actions`.
`data-position` picks a corner. The panel is also available as
`c15t.devtools` in the console. Remove the tag before you publish, because the
panel shows internal state to every visitor.

## Why is there no banner?

Check the Network tab for the request to your backend's `/init` endpoint.

- **The request failed with a CORS error.** Add your site's exact origin,
  including `https://` and any `www`, to the trusted origins in your Inth
  project.
- **The request returned 200.** The policy for your location may not ask for a
  banner. Many policies show none outside regions with consent laws. Open the
  DevTools Location tab and try a country such as `DE`.
- **There is no request.** The tag did not run. Check that the `src` loads,
  that no other script on the page threw first, and that the tag has no
  `data-manual`. A Content Security Policy must allow the CDN in `script-src`
  and your backend in `connect-src`.
- **You already chose.** A stored choice hides the banner. Open a private
  window, or clear the `c15t` cookie and localStorage key.

## Why does a vendor script run before a choice?

The script tag only holds scripts that are `type="text/plain"` with a
`data-c15t-category`. Search the page source for the vendor's domain. A second
copy added by a theme setting, a plugin or a tag manager runs on its own terms.
Remove it, or gate it too.

Check the category as well. Under an opt-out policy, optional categories are
allowed until the visitor refuses, so vendors load on the first visit by
design.

## Why does the console say "call c15t.init() first"?

Something called a `window.c15t` method before the client existed. Either the
call was queued with `c15t.push()` as a method other than `config`, `on` or
`onInit`, or the tag has `data-manual` and nothing called `c15t.init()`. Move
the call into an `on('ready', ...)` listener, or wait for `c15t.ready()`.

## Why is my queued configuration ignored?

Look for an inline script that assigns `window.c15t = [...]`. Assignment
replaces calls other scripts queued before it. Use
`window.c15t = window.c15t || []` followed by `c15t.push(...)` everywhere.
A `config` call made after the bundle ran also does nothing; it has to run
before the tag, or be passed to `c15t.init()` with `data-manual`.

## Why does the page throw "Load c15t.iab.js instead of c15t.js"?

Two c15t bundles are on the page. Keep only `c15t.iab.js` for an IAB policy,
and remove `c15t.js` or `c15t.headless.js`, including copies a plugin adds.

## Why did the banner change after a release?

`@alpha` in the tag's URL follows the newest prerelease. Replace it with an
exact version so your page changes only when you change it.
