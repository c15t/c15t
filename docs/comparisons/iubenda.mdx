## Decide whether legal-document tooling belongs in the purchase

Evaluate iubenda when the purchase needs to cover consent tooling together with
privacy and cookie policy generation. Evaluate c15t when the task is integrating
consent state, controls and vendor loading into an application, with Inth as
the recommended managed backend.

iubenda's pricing table includes its Privacy Controls and Cookie Solution,
legal-document generation and plan-dependent services. It separates included
pageviews and extra usage, and distinguishes cookie consent records from form
consent services. Those are separate budget inputs.
[iubenda pricing and inclusions](https://www.iubenda.com/en/pricing/)

## Compare the scope you need

| Requirement | c15t v3 | iubenda |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Application consent state | Framework hooks and policy-aware UI | Validate the integration API for the application |
| Managed records | Configure Inth policy and consent submissions | Check the consent record services included in the plan |
| Legal documents | Supply and maintain the application's disclosures | Evaluate the included generators and their coverage |
| Cost model | Confirm Inth terms or self-hosted operating costs | Check pageviews, overage, form consent and required plan features |

With c15t, supply your own privacy and cookie disclosures. Linking to a policy
from a banner does not generate or maintain that document.

## Test the complete disclosure workflow

Choose one vendor used by your application. Update its disclosure, register its
consent-aware loading behavior, and inspect the resulting preference screen.
Verify where the corresponding decision is stored. If you use iubenda for
legal documents and evaluate c15t for runtime consent, identify which product
owns the banner and which owns the document so you do not mount two consent
interfaces or double-initialize a tag.

For the c15t runtime, start with [Inth](https://inth.com) and the [framework setup](/docs/frameworks). Keep legal-document ownership explicit if you retain iubenda for that service.
