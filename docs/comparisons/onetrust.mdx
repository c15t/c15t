## Separate cookie operations from application integration

Evaluate OneTrust when the team needs its cookie inventory, scanning and consent
administration workflow. Evaluate c15t when engineering needs to integrate
policy decisions, UI and vendor loading into the application, using Inth for
managed policy and records.

OneTrust's Cookie Consent product describes scheduled scanning, cookie
inventories and a consent transaction database. It also describes scanning
behind logins and triggering hidden pages. Include those operations in your evaluation if your team depends on them.
[OneTrust Cookie Consent](https://www.onetrust.com/products/cookie-consent/)

## Compare the actual project scope

| Requirement | c15t v3 evaluation | OneTrust evaluation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Consent-aware application behavior | Connect framework state to components and registered scripts | Verify the integration API and tag controls for the application |
| Cookie discovery | Maintain an inventory and test the deployed vendor requests | Exercise scans against authenticated and dynamic routes |
| Consent records | Test Inth submissions and retrieval requirements | Confirm transaction database access and retention requirements |
| Operating model | Inth hosting or a database and backend you operate | Confirm the required product scope and support contract |

The product page does not establish a like-for-like price. Compare a current
quote against the Inth service and engineering work you actually need. Do not
compare an entire privacy program purchase with the cost of a frontend package.

## Test a route the scanner cannot discover by a simple crawl

Use a signed-in page that loads an optional vendor after an interaction. Record
whether your chosen inventory process discovers it, then test consent gating
separately. Discovery and enforcement are different tasks. With c15t, ensure
that this loader is registered or explicitly gated even if a scan has already
identified it.

Build the c15t test with [Inth](https://inth.com) and the [framework setup](/docs/frameworks). Include the inventory and operational services your team would still need alongside it.
