## Attentive

Attentive shows SMS and email signup units and tracks on-site events for its marketing messages. Waits for `marketing` consent.

<include src="../shared/examples/javascript/attentive-configuration.mdx" />

| Option | Type | Required | Description |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `siteSlug` | `string` | Yes | Site slug from your Attentive tag URL, `https://cdn.attn.tv/<siteSlug>/dtag.js`. Ask your Attentive contact if you don't have it. |
| `scriptUrl` | `string` | No | Exact tag URL from Attentive or your proxy. |

**When consent is withdrawn:** c15t reloads the page, and the next page does not load the tag. Attentive has no supported way to stop a running tag, so keep `reloadOnConsentRevoked` on.

- Some Attentive identifiers, such as `__attentive_id`, last up to 390 days. The reload does not delete them.
