The snapshot is fixed at build time:

- Rebuild after changing policies, translations or vendors. If your CI caches
  build output, force a fresh build.
- The build must reach the backend. A failed fetch stops Next.js, TanStack
  Start, SvelteKit and Vite builds. By default, Astro and Nuxt log a warning
  instead and fetch the policy at runtime.
- Consent choices still go to the backend.
