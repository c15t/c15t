`consentManifest()` from `c15t/vue/vite` downloads the policy during
`vite build`, when the app uses `manifest()`, and in `vite dev` when the app
first loads it:

<include src="../examples/vue/vite-config.mdx" />

Without a `backendURL` option, the plugin reads `VITE_C15T_BACKEND_URL`, then
`VITE_INTH_PROJECT_URL`, from the environment or `.env`. `manifest()` from `c15t/vue/vue-plugin`, with no
options, reads the downloaded policy and the same backend URL, so `src/main.ts`
needs no import from `c15t/generated`:

<include src="../examples/vue/main.mdx" />

The browser resolves the visitor's policy from the bundled snapshot, with no
`/manifest` request. When the policy depends on the visitor's country or
region and neither `inputs` nor `geoURL` supplies one, the browser asks the
backend's `/init` instead. The app bundles the policy and English copy, and
loads the resolver as its own chunk when it needs it.

When the build has no snapshot, as in `vite dev` after a failed download,
`manifest()` fetches `${backendURL}/manifest` when the app starts.

For policy edits without a rebuild, use
[`manifest({ source: 'runtime' })`](/docs/frameworks/vue/rendering#fetch-the-manifest-at-runtime),
or [`hosted()`](/docs/frameworks/vue/rendering#ask-the-backend-for-each-visitors-policy)
when you need the backend's geolocation.
