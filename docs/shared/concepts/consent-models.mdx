<section id="overview">
  c15t has three permission models: `opt-in`, `opt-out` and `iab`. `PolicyRule.model`
  sets permission defaults. `PolicyRule.prompt` independently selects `choice`,
  `notice` or `none`; only opt-out permits notice or none.

  Use `effectivePermissions` for processing and `promptRequirement` for outstanding
  interaction. Defaults, receipt presence and prompt visibility are different facts.
</section>

<section id="models-explained">
  ## Permission models

  Opt-in denies optional categories without current positive authority. Opt-out
  allows in-scope categories by default, subject to denials and privacy restrictions.
  IAB targets require confirmed, valid TCF 2.3 authority as well as applicable
  category restrictions. A category acceptance never creates vendor authority.

  Strict scope denies excluded categories. Explicit denials and standing opt-outs
  remain effective after positive grants expire. These rules apply to scripts,
  frames, network blocking and Google Consent Mode.
</section>

<section id="jurisdiction-mapping">
  ## Resolution fallback

  A country name does not authorize processing. Unconfigured, no-match and failed
  resolution all select a safe opt-in choice rule. Their statuses remain distinct.
  An intentional no-prompt deployment must configure opt-out plus none explicitly.
  See [policy packs](/docs/frameworks/react/concepts/policy-packs) for preset
  assumptions and dated source reviews.
</section>
