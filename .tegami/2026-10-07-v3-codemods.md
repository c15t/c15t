### Add codemods for the rest of the v2 to v3 upgrade

`c15t codemods` gains eleven v3 transforms that run when you name them:

- `consent-provider-options` renames `ConsentManagerProvider` and its types to `ConsentProvider`, turns `mode` with `backendURL`, `headers` and `customFetch` into `hosted()`, turns offline mode and `offlinePolicy.policyPacks` into `offline({ policyRules })`, and renames `iframeBlockerConfig` to `iframeBlocker`.
- `root-exports-to-subpaths` moves imports of names that left the `c15t/react` and `c15t/next` roots to `/headless`, `/consent-dialog-trigger`, `/types` and `/components/consent-banner`.
- `dev-tools-to-c15t` points `@c15t/dev-tools/react` and `/tanstack` at `c15t/next/devtools` or `c15t/react/devtools` and drops the `namespace` prop.
- `policy-packs-to-policy-rules` renames `policyPackPresets` to `policyRulePresets` and `worldNoBanner()` to `worldNone()`.
- `css-variables-to-v3` renames `--consent-widget-*` and `--frame-*` in stylesheets and inline styles.
- `postcss-tailwind3` adds the c15t plugin before `tailwindcss` in an object-form `postcss.config` for Tailwind CSS 3.
- `callbacks-to-v3`, `theme-to-consent-theme` and `iab-option-to-iab-provider` rename what they can and mark callbacks, theme tokens and the `iab` option for manual work.
- `node-sdk-to-v3` moves `@c15t/node-sdk` code to `createC15tClient()` and the v3 option and method names.
- `backend-config-to-v3` moves `policyPacks`, `branding`, `customTranslations`, `i18n` and `appName` under `manifest` in a `@c15t/backend` config.

Where a change needs a decision, the codemods leave a `TODO(c15t v3)` comment, and most keep the v2 code so type-checking fails until you finish it. Run them with `--dry-run --json` first to review the proposed files.
