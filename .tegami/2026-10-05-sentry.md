### Add a Sentry integration

`sentry()` from `@c15t/integrations/sentry` loads Sentry from its CDN, or works with the Sentry SDK your app already runs. Session Replay and user data wait for measurement consent, and error monitoring can too. The CLI offers it in the integration picker.
