### Add `c15t/astro` to the `c15t` package

Astro sites can install `c15t` and import `c15t/astro`. Components are at
`c15t/astro/components/<name>.astro` and stylesheets at
`c15t/astro/styles.css` and `c15t/astro/iab/styles.css`.

`@c15t/astro` exports `./components/consent-script.astro`, builds under pnpm
when a site lists only `c15t`, and marks its `astro` peer optional, so
installing `c15t` for other frameworks does not pull in Astro.
