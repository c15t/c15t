### Add IAB Global Privacy Platform support

`@c15t/iab/gpp` adds `createGPP()`, which installs the GPP 1.1 CMP API
(`__gpp`) against a consent kernel and keeps its GPP string in step with the
visitor's choices. The matched policy rule decides whether a section
applies: under an `iab` rule, the TC String the TCF CMP confirmed becomes the
`tcfeuv2` section; a rule with the `preferences` or `opt-out` right gives US
visitors their state section (`usca`, `usva`, `usco` and 13 others), or the
MSPA US National section when the region is unknown, the state has no
section, or `usApproach` is `'national'`. A rule without those rights gets no
section. Sale, sharing and targeted advertising opt-outs follow the
`marketing` category, notices follow the rule's rights, and the GPC signal is
reported where the section has a GPC subsection.

The API answers `ping`, `addEventListener`, `removeEventListener`,
`hasSection`, `getSection` and `getField`, takes over calls queued by a GPP
stub, and serves iframes through the `__gppLocator` frame. `initializeGPPStub()`
installs a stub early. Indiana, Kentucky, Maryland and Rhode Island sections
are not encoded yet; visitors there get the US National section.
