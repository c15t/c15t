About this demo and the exemption

This demo runs the workspace consent kernel and the new exemption policy. Integrations are simulated; no analytics or advertising requests are sent. Each location has a separate visitor whose preferences survive reloading.

The example assumes aggregate statistics used only to improve this service, with clear information, restricted sharing and a free way to object. An analytics product name does not establish eligibility.

Turn this off to see a policy change. Statistics then require consent; permission from the exemption does not become a consent grant.
