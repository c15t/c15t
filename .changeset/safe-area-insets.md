Keep the consent surfaces out of the system-reserved bands. On a notched iPhone the banner's buttons sat under the home indicator and a sheet had nothing keeping its heading below the status bar, because nothing in the built-in components read an inset. `ConsentBanner` now lifts its bottom edge out of the bottom band, narrows to the side bands a notch sets in landscape, and keeps a full interaction row between the band and its controls, so a swipe up at the indicator cannot land on a button; `ConsentDialog` and `ConsentPreferences` inset the layer their sheet sits in on every band, and the scrollable body is capped against the inset height rather than the window, so the sheet cannot grow tall enough to put its own heading by the clock. Rotation and the collapsing iOS home indicator re-layout without a remount, and the banner stays an absolutely positioned layer, so mounting one still moves no app content.

`C15tProvider` takes an optional `safeAreaInsets` prop for the live measurement. An app already running `react-native-safe-area-context` forwards `useSafeAreaInsets()` and gets exact bands on all four edges. Nothing new is installed and no peer is required: the package imports no inset module, because Metro resolves static imports when it bundles, so a conditional read of an optional peer would fail the build for a host that skipped it. `useConsentSafeArea()` returns the bands in force with `measured` saying whether they came from the host or from the floor the package reserves when nobody measured: the 44-point interaction minimum the theme already enforces, which is wider than the widest home-indicator inset Apple ships, plus `StatusBar.currentHeight` for the top band on Android. Mount the built-in surfaces with no new code and the controls stay out of the indicator; forward a measurement and the layout stops guessing.
